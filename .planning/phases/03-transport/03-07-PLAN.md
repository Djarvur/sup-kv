## Artifacts this phase produces

See 03-01-PLAN.md for the consolidated phase list. This plan adds: `listingQueue`/`listingInFlight` with broadcast round rotation and per-peer rotation in `peer`; `recordSend` queue with `(key, version)` dedupe and a record pacer; deterministic `jitterOffset`/`jitteredSpacing` helpers; bounded `egressKey{handle, kind, key}` queue with `maxEgressEntries = 32768`, drop-oldest plus dedupe-map deletion; `QueuedSends()` total-queue accessor; `internal/app/repl/egress_test.go`.

<objective>
Pace everything the transport emits — listings, record sends/retransmits, reactive answers — rotate the listing start, jitter the right timers, and bound the answer queue.

Purpose: R-01's systematic tail loss and R-02's retransmit storm both come from unpaced whole-set emission; XPORT-07 additionally requires deduplicated, bounded answers. This plan implements locked decisions D-01, D-05 and D-08's queue half, and the XPORT-01/XPORT-02(2)(3)/XPORT-07 acceptance mechanisms.
Output: three paced streams sharing the normalized interval, rotating listings, deterministic jitter, a bounded deduped egress queue, and tests that pin pacing, rotation and dedupe without touching the red tests.
</objective>
