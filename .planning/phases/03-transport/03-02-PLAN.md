## Artifacts this phase produces

See 03-01-PLAN.md for the consolidated phase list. This plan adds: `storage.Store.Version`, `storage.Store.Lookup`, `storage.Store.Versions`, `storage.MaxKeyBytes` (1024), `Engine.Lookup`, `Engine.Version`, `Engine.Versions`, `internal/app/storage/view_test.go`, and the WR-02 fix in `simnet.Cluster.Write`/`Delete`.

<objective>
Give the replication core a cheap, tombstone-visible read view — per-key version lookup, a single-record point read, and a shallow key→version snapshot — and fix the simulator's swallowed `Set` error.

Purpose: every listing, `DataRequest` reply and `DataSync` comparison currently takes a full-store deep copy (`Records()`), measured at 2.3 ms / 10.9 MiB per datagram at 10 000 × 1 KiB. This plan lands the store half of the fix (XPORT-11) and the WR-02 hygiene fix that also removes a full-store copy per simulated write. It implements locked decision D-08's store half.
Output: three read methods with tombstone visibility, the single key-limit constant, Engine forwarding, and WR-02's error-checked simulator write path.
</objective>
