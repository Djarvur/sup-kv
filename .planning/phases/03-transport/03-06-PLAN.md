## Artifacts this phase produces

See 03-01-PLAN.md for the consolidated phase list. This plan adds: `port.Result`, `ResultKind` (`ResultAck`, `ResultDrained`, `ResultSuperseded`, `ResultWaitSetEmpty`), `Outbox.Ack/Drained/Superseded/WaitSetEmpty/DrainResults`; the adapter tracker with `WaitReplicated`; `LocalNotify`/`NotifyLocalWrite` `track bool`; `ServerOptions.WaitReplicated` replacing `PendingCopy`; the thin `wait.go`. It removes: `mirrorState`, `retainDrained`, `prevPending`, `lastAck`, `drainedRetention`, `Adapter.PendingCopy`, `ServerOptions.PendingCopy`, and the `waitPoll`/`waitAppear` polling machinery.

**Footprint note (scope_sanity, accepted):** 17 modified files, but the spread is one mechanical seam propagation — the `LocalNotify`/`WaitReplicated` signature changes its producer (`server.go`), its adapter implementation (`udprepl/repl.go`), its consumers (`wait.go`, `gate.go`, `main.go`) and every test that constructs or drives the seam. The three tasks stay within the 95k estimate; splitting would separate a seam from its call sites and leave the tree uncompilable between plans. The plan summary records this composition.

<objective>
Turn "record acknowledged by peer X" into an Outbox result consumed by a waiter tracker, and delete the mirror-plus-poll machinery.

Purpose: `wait_for_replication` currently polls a copy-on-write mirror every 5 ms, and the adapter rebuilds that mirror after every event (the measured 52% CPU site). This plan implements locked decision D-04: results flow out of the core through the existing Outbox; the adapter owns waiting; `wait.go` becomes a thin, honest projection. This is the seam Phase 4/5 and APIS-01 build on.
Output: four result events, the adapter tracker, the signature change through gRPC and main, the polling removal, and preserved wait semantics under rewritten tests.
</objective>
