<objective>
Reconciliation both directions: the DataRequest reply path (REPL-04) and the chunked
DataSync anti-entropy machinery (REPL-05) — sorted listings chunked to sync_chunk_bytes
with a conservative core-side wire-size estimate, startup and timer triggers, per-key
compare against a single snapshot, and one rate-limited egress queue for all reactive
unicasts.

Purpose: after a partition, a restart, or an empty disk, nodes discover and repair every
divergence through the same deterministic machinery — bounded by sync_response_rate so an
empty node's recovery cannot become a flood.
Output: expanded internal/app/repl/core.go (DataRequest handling, chunking, sync triggers,
compare, egress limiter with QueuedResponses accessor), sync_test.go, a cross-seam wire-size
test in udprepl, and the sync_response_rate rounding rule echoed in docs/IDEA.md §Parameters.
</objective>
