<objective>

The review's 20 000 × 61 440-byte load paid ~2.4 GB of transient garbage for nothing.

Purpose: PERF-05 explicitly includes this cleanup ("the paced replay path must not copy `rec.Data` twice before discarding it"). The fix is two deleted clones plus two corrected seam contracts, proven by a deterministic version-only assertion and an allocation gate at the adapter — not by a benchmark that swallows the copy.
Output: both seams carry a version-only record, two internal tests pin it, and the seam docs say so.
</objective>
