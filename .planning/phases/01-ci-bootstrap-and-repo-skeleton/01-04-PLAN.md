## Phase Goal

Quality gates and the enforced repo skeleton exist before any feature code, so every subsequent change lands inside the architecture and behind a red-light-blocking gate.

*(This plan delivers the goal's enforcement half: the server-side ruleset that turns the green matrix into a merge precondition and forbids direct pushes, force pushes, and deletion on master.)*
