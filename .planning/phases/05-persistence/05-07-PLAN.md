## Artifacts this phase produces

Consolidated list for the whole phase (later plans add to it in their own sections). This plan creates:
`persist.Config.Now`, `Config.After`, `Config.OnDumpFailure`, `defaultRetryBase`, `defaultRetryCap`, `nextRetry`, the scheduler's retry channel, `DumpStats.DiskFull`/`Permission`, the `supkv_dumps_failures_total` family, `simnet.Cluster.NotifyInstalled`, and `internal/app/repl/sim_dump_test.go` with `virtualAfter`.

<objective>
Put the dumper's time behind the same injected seam as the replication core and make dump failures decay honestly — PERSIST-12 and PERSIST-05 — then use the seam to exercise dump↔replication in the simulator.

Purpose: today the engine and the scheduler read the wall clock directly and the retry cadence is the 50 ms debounce forever, so a persistent ENOSPC busy-loops at 18 attempts/s with no log and no class. `Config.Now`/`Config.After` mirror the repl core's time-arrives-from-outside discipline, letting the simulator drive the dump loop on virtual time; the retry ladder grows to a 5 s cap, each failed attempt emits one ERROR through `OnDumpFailure`, and the adapter's errno classification crosses the port as a class.
Output: the injected clock/timer seam, the backoff ladder, the failure classes and counters, the EACCES process proof, the additive `Cluster.NotifyInstalled` harness seam, and the seeded simulator scenario.
</objective>
