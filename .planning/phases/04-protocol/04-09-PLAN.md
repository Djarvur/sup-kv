## Artifacts this phase produces

This plan adds: `notificationsRegressed` + `NotificationsRegressed()`, the strict-older notification guard, `peer.seenRuns` (bounded 8-entry ring), the alternation branch in `handleHello`, and the shared per-`(node_id, class)` rate-limited WARN helper used by the duplicate-node-id paths and `discardValidated`.

<objective>
Stop regressive local notifications from reaching the wire, and name the alternating-identity misconfiguration instead of treating it as an endless series of restarts.

Purpose: R-10's failure is a stale local notification that broadcasts a version the node has already superseded — peers see a regression and may answer, amplifying traffic. R-12's failure is a third process flapping under a known `node_id`: today every new run_id is a restart, so a hostile or misconfigured host can force a full listing per datagram. Both are decision rules over state the core already holds.
Output: the guarded notification with its counter, and the bounded seen-run discrimination with the Spec's configuration-error WARN.
</objective>
