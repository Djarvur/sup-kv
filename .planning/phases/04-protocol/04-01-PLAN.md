## Artifacts this phase produces

Consolidated list for the whole phase (later plans add to it in their own sections). This plan creates:
`DataSync.SyncID`/`ChunkIndex`/`ChunkCount` (port + wire), `DecodeStatus ProtoVersionMismatch`, `repl.SupportedProtoVersion = 2`, `repl.DiscardBadSyncHeader` + `DiscardStats.BadSyncHeader`, core `syncStreamKey`/`syncStream`, `nextSyncID`, `syncs`, `listedByPeer`, `listingGen`, `listingComplete`, `maxSyncStreams`, `maxSyncEntriesPerStream`, `maxSyncEntriesTotal`, `maxSyncChunks`, accessors `ListingGeneration`/`ListingComplete`/`ListedKeysSnapshot`/`SyncStreamDropped`, adapter `listingMirror`, `Adapter.ListedKey`, `listingGen` adapter field, `scanDiscardKey{nodeID, class}`.

<objective>
Make "the listing is complete" a real, wire-carried fact and move the proto-version verdict ahead of the body decode — the architectural spine the rest of Phase 4 hangs on.

Purpose: R-03's defect is that the first `DataSync` chunk is read as the whole listing; the fix is a completeness header plus per-stream accumulation, exposed to the gate through one atomic mirror so "gate open" cannot precede "listed keys visible". PROTO-07 belongs here because the proto version must change for the header to be trustworthy, and a future version's body may not follow this version's CRC contract.
Output: the v2 wire schema and regenerated code, the header/accumulation core, the listing mirror and `ListedKey`, the pre-body verdict with its class and per-peer WARN limit, and the re-derived chunk-budget fixtures.
</objective>
